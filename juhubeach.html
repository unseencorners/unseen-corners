🌅 Juhu Beach, Mumbai

Juhu Beach is Mumbai’s most famous and lively coastal destination. Known for its golden sands, stunning sunsets, and vibrant street food, it offers a perfect blend of relaxation and city buzz.

🏖️ Highlights & Facts

  • Popular for Mumbai’s iconic street snacks like pav bhaji and bhel puri.
  • Frequented by locals, tourists, and Bollywood celebrities.
  • Perfect for morning jogs, camel rides, and beachside photography.
  • Beautiful sunset views over the Arabian Sea.

🕒 Visitor Information

Timings:
Open all day (best time: evening sunset).
Location:
Juhu, Mumbai, Maharashtra, India.
Best Time:
October – March (pleasant weather).
