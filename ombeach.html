🏖️ Om Beach, Gokarna

Om Beach, named for its natural Om-shaped shoreline, is one of Karnataka’s most iconic beaches. With golden sands, gentle waves, and a peaceful atmosphere, it’s a perfect escape for both adventure and relaxation.

🌊 Highlights & Activities

  • Named after the spiritual “Om” symbol formed by its twin crescents.
  • Popular for kayaking, banana boat rides, and parasailing.
  • Ideal for meditation and beach camping.
  • Nearby cafes offer fresh seafood and sunset views.

🕒 Visitor Information

Timings:
Open 24 hours (best visited at sunrise or sunset).
Location:
Gokarna, Karnataka, India.
Best Time:
October – March (ideal weather for beach activities).
