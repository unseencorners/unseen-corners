🏖️ Kovalam Beach, Kerala

Kovalam Beach, located near Thiruvananthapuram, is one of India’s most famous seaside destinations. Known for its golden sands, calm waves, and iconic lighthouse, it offers an ideal blend of relaxation, adventure, and coastal charm.

🌅 Highlights & Facts

  • Renowned for its three crescent-shaped beaches: Lighthouse, Hawah, and Samudra.
  • Popular for surfing, sunbathing, and Ayurvedic wellness centers.
  • The lighthouse provides panoramic views of the Arabian Sea.
  • Famous for vibrant coastal cuisine and cultural festivals.

🕒 Visitor Information

Timings:
Open all day (best visits during sunrise & sunset)
Location:
Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala, India
Best Time:
September – March (pleasant weather for swimming & sightseeing)

🗓 Suggested Itinerary

Day 1 - Morning: Relax on Lighthouse Beach and climb the lighthouse.

Day 1 - Evening: Watch the sunset and shop for souvenirs at Kovalam market.
Day 2 - Morning: Experience an Ayurvedic massage at a local spa.
Day 2 - Night: Dine while listening to the ocean waves.
