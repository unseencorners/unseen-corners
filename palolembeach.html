🌴 Palolem Beach, Goa

Palolem Beach is one of South Goa’s most serene and scenic beaches, famous for its crescent shape, calm turquoise waters, and laid-back tropical vibe. Surrounded by coconut palms and beach huts, it’s ideal for swimming, kayaking, and peaceful getaways.

🌅 Highlights & Facts

Known for its beautiful crescent shape and golden sands.
Popular for dolphin spotting, kayaking, and yoga retreats.
Silent discos and beach huts create a unique nightlife.
Close to Agonda and Patnem beaches.

🕒 Visitor Information

Timings: Open 24 hours
Location: Canacona, South Goa
Best Time: November – February

🗓 Suggested Itinerary
