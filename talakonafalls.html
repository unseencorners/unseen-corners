🌄 Talakona Waterfalls, Andhra Pradesh

Talakona is the highest waterfall in Andhra Pradesh, located in Sri Venkateswara National Park, and is known for its lush forest, natural beauty, and spiritual aura.

📜 About the Waterfall

  • Falls from a height of ~270 feet (82 m), the tallest in the state.
  • Surrounded by dense forests rich in medicinal herbs and wildlife.
  • The nearby Lord Siddheswara Swamy Temple attracts devotees.
  • Declared a biosphere reserve due to its ecological importance.

🕒 Visiting Information

Timings:
6:00 AM – 7:30 PM
Location:
Near Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh
Best Time:
September to January
