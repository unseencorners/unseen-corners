🏔️ Kangchenjunga – The Third Highest Peak

Kangchenjunga is the third highest mountain in the world, located in the Himalayas on the border between Nepal and India, known for its remote and pristine beauty.

📜 History & Significance

  • Renowned for its sacred status in local cultures.
  • Known for difficult climbs and rugged terrain.
  • Home to rare Himalayan flora and fauna.
  • A symbol of natural grandeur and adventure.

🕒 Visiting Information

Best Climbing Season:
April–May and October–November.
Location:
Eastern Himalayas, Nepal/India border.
Activities:
Trekking, mountaineering, cultural tours.
