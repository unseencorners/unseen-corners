🛕 Madurai Meenakshi Temple

The Meenakshi Temple, located in Madurai, Tamil Nadu, is a historic Hindu temple dedicated to Goddess Meenakshi (a form of Parvati) and Lord Sundareswarar (Shiva). It is renowned for its stunning architecture and cultural significance.

📜 History – When Was It Built?

  • The temple's origins date back over 2,000 years, with significant expansions during the Pandya dynasty.
  • It is famous for its 14 colorful gopurams (gateway towers) adorned with thousands of sculptures.
  • The current structure was largely built during the Nayak period in the 16th and 17th centuries.
  • It is a major pilgrimage site and a symbol of Tamil culture and heritage.

🕒 Visiting Information

Timings:
5:00 AM – 10:00 PM daily.
Location:
Madurai, Tamil Nadu, India.
Best Time:
November to February, especially during the Meenakshi Thirukalyanam festival.
