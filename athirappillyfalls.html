💦 Athirappilly Waterfalls

Athirappilly Waterfalls, often called the "Niagara of India," is the largest waterfall in Kerala, cascading down 80 feet from the Chalakudy River amidst the lush Sholayar forests.

📖 Highlights & Facts

  • Height: 80 feet, width over 330 feet during monsoon.
  • Located on the Chalakudy River, Thrissur district.
  • Famous shooting spot for many Indian films.
  • Nearby attractions: Vazhachal Falls and Charpa Falls.

🕒 Visitor Information

Timings:
8:00 AM – 6:00 PM
Location:
Chalakudy, Thrissur, Kerala
Best Time:
June – October & December – February
