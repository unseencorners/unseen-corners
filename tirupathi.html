🛕 Tirumala Venkateswara Temple

The Tirumala Venkateswara Temple in Andhra Pradesh is one of the most famous Hindu temples dedicated to Lord Venkateswara, a form of Lord Vishnu. It attracts millions of devotees annually.

📜 History – When Was It Built?

  • The temple's origins date back over a thousand years, with significant contributions from various dynasties like the Pallavas and Vijayanagara Empire.
  • It is one of the richest temples in the world by donations received.
  • The temple architecture reflects Dravidian style.
  • It is a major pilgrimage center for devotees across India.

🕒 Visiting Information

Timings:
3:00 AM – 10:00 PM daily.
Location:
Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh, India.
Best Time:
September to January, especially during Brahmotsavam festival.
