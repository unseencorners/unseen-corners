🏞️ Anamudi – The Highest Peak of South India

Anamudi, at 2,695 m (8,842 ft), is the highest peak in South India, located in the Western Ghats within the Eravikulam National Park, Kerala.

📜 History & Significance

  • First ascent recorded in the early 19th century by British surveyors.
  • A biodiversity hotspot with rare flora and fauna.
  • Popular trekking destination within Eravikulam National Park.
  • Culturally significant to local tribes and communities.

🕒 Visiting Information

Best Trekking Season:
October–March.
Location:
Idukki District, Kerala, India (Western Ghats).
Activities:
Trekking, wildlife spotting, photography.
