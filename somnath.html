🛕 Somnath Temple

Somnath Temple, located in Gujarat, is one of the twelve Jyotirlinga shrines of Lord Shiva. It is renowned for its rich history, stunning architecture, and spiritual significance as a symbol of resilience and devotion.

📜 History – When Was It Built?

  • The temple's origins date back to ancient times, with many reconstructions after multiple invasions.
  • It stands as a symbol of Hindu resilience and cultural heritage.
  • Famous for its Chalukya style architecture and intricate carvings.
  • A vital pilgrimage site for devotees of Lord Shiva and Indian history enthusiasts alike.

🕒 Visiting Information

Timings:
6:00 AM – 9:00 PM daily.
Location:
Veraval, Gujarat, India.
Best Time:
October to March, during festivals like Maha Shivaratri and Diwali.
