🌊 Shivanasamudra Waterfalls

Shivanasamudra, located in Mandya district of Karnataka, is a majestic segmented waterfall formed by the Kaveri River. It divides into two streams — Gaganachukki and Bharachukki — creating a breathtaking natural wonder amidst rocky terrain.

📖 Origin & Significance

  • The name "Shivanasamudra" means “Sea of Lord Shiva”.
  • The site houses one of Asia’s first hydroelectric power stations (built in 1902).
  • It’s a popular picnic spot and a major tourist attraction near Mysuru.

🕒 Visitor Information

Timings:
