🌄 Dodda Beta – Majestic Peak of Coorg

Dodda Beta is the highest peak in Coorg, Karnataka, India, known for its lush greenery and panoramic views of the Western Ghats.

📜 History & Significance

  • Popular trekking destination with rich biodiversity.
  • Known for spectacular sunrise and sunset views.
  • Home to diverse flora and fauna of the Western Ghats.
  • Important cultural spot for local Kodava communities.

🕒 Visiting Information

Best Trekking Season:
October–February.
Location:
Coorg, Karnataka, India (Western Ghats).
Activities:
Trekking, bird watching, nature photography.
