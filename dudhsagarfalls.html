🌊 Dudhsagar Waterfalls

Dudhsagar Falls, meaning “Sea of Milk,” is one of India’s tallest and most spectacular waterfalls, located on the Mandovi River along the Goa–Karnataka border. The four-tiered cascade creates a misty, milky illusion as it plunges from a height of over 310 meters.

📖 Highlights & Facts

  • One of India’s tallest waterfalls at 310 meters.
  • Located within the Bhagwan Mahavir Wildlife Sanctuary.
  • Best visited during monsoon when the falls are at their peak glory.
  • Famous for the railway bridge passing in front of the waterfall.

🕒 Visitor Information

Timings:
8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
Location:

Best Time:
