🌈 Seven Sisters Waterfalls

The Seven Sisters Waterfalls, also known as Nohsngithiang Falls, is one of the most spectacular natural wonders of Meghalaya.

📖 Highlights & Facts

  • Located in Cherrapunji (Sohra), one of the wettest places on Earth.
  • The seven streams symbolize the seven states of Northeast India.
  • The falls are most stunning during the monsoon season (June–September).

🕒 Visitor Information

Timings:
6:00 AM – 6:00 PM
Location:
Mawsmai, near Cherrapunji, Meghalaya
Best Time:
June – September
