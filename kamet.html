🏔️ Kamet – The Giant of Garhwal Himalaya

Kamet, at 7,756 m (25,446 ft), is the third-highest mountain in the Garhwal region of Uttarakhand, India. It is known for its majestic peak and challenging climbs.

🕒 Visiting Information

Best Trekking Season:
May–June and September–October.
Location:
Uttarakhand, India (Chamoli District).
Activities:
Mountaineering, trekking, wildlife photography, cultural visits.
