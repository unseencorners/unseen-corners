🏖️ Ramakrishna Beach, Visakhapatnam

Ramakrishna Beach, affectionately known as RK Beach, is one of the most famous and scenic beaches of Andhra Pradesh. Stretching along the Bay of Bengal, it’s a perfect blend of calm waves, cultural charm, and city vibes.

🌊 Highlights & Activities

  • Enjoy the serene sunrise and scenic sea promenade.
  • Visit nearby attractions like the Submarine Museum and Kali Temple.
  • Perfect for beach walks, photography, and picnics.
  • Clean, well-maintained, and family-friendly beach destination.

🕒 Visitor Information

Timings:
Open all day (ideal for sunrise & evening visits).
Location:
Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh, India.
Best Time:
November – February for pleasant weather.
