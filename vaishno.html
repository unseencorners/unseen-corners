🛕 Vaishno Devi

Vaishno Devi, located in Jammu and Kashmir, is one of the holiest Hindu pilgrimage sites dedicated to Goddess Durga. The cave shrine attracts millions every year with its spiritual energy and scenic Himalayan surroundings.

📜 History – When Was It Established?

  • The temple dates back to ancient times, believed to have been established by the Pandavas during the Mahabharata era.
  • It symbolizes the divine feminine energy and faith of millions of devotees.
  • Situated at a height of 5200 feet on the Trikuta Hills, the pilgrimage involves a 12 km trek.
  • The shrine is a major spiritual and cultural landmark in Northern India.

🕒 Visiting Information

Timings:
5:00 AM – 8:00 PM daily.
Location:
Katra, Jammu and Kashmir, India.
Best Time:
March to November; avoid heavy snowfall months.
