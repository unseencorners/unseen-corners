🏔️ Dhauladhar Hills

The Dhauladhar Hills, a majestic sub-range of the Himalayas in Himachal Pradesh, are known for their snow-clad peaks, serene monasteries, and breathtaking trekking routes. From Dharamshala to Triund, every view offers a glimpse of nature’s perfection.

🌄 Highlights & Attractions

  • Triund Trek – a scenic trail offering stunning views of the Kangra Valley.
  • McLeod Ganj – home to the Dalai Lama and Tibetan culture.
  • Bhagsu Waterfall and Bhagsunath Temple nearby.
  • Paragliding and mountain camping experiences.

🕒 Visitor Information

Timings:
Open all year; ideal for treks from March to June.
Location:
Kangra District, Himachal Pradesh, India.
Best Time:
March – June and September – November.
