🏔️ Trisul – Majestic Peak in the Kumaon Himalayas

Trisul is a group of three Himalayan mountain peaks in Uttarakhand. Trisul I, the highest among them, stands at 7,120 meters (23,359 ft), offering a breathtaking view and historical significance in mountaineering.

📜 About Trisul

  • Located in the Kumaon region of Uttarakhand, India.
  • Named after "Trishul" (Trident) – weapon of Lord Shiva.
  • Trisul I was the first peak over 7,000 m climbed by any expedition (in 1907).
  • Visible from Auli and other parts of the Garhwal and Kumaon Himalayas.

🕒 Visiting Details

Best Time to Visit:
April – June & September – November
Location:
Uttarakhand, Kumaon Himalayas
Notes:
Trekking requires permission; suitable for experienced trekkers.
