💦 Jog Waterfalls

Jog Waterfalls, located in the Shimoga district of Karnataka, is India’s second-highest plunge waterfall, cascading majestically from a height of 830 feet. Fed by the Sharavathi River, the falls split into four streams – Raja, Roarer, Rocket, and Rani – offering a breathtaking view amidst the lush Western Ghats.

📖 Highlights & Facts

  • Height: 830 feet (253 meters) – one of India’s tallest waterfalls.
  • Formed by the Sharavathi River.
  • Comprises four distinct cascades: Raja, Rani, Roarer, and Rocket.
  • Best viewed during monsoon when the water volume peaks.

🕒 Visitor Information

Timings:
