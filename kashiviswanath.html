🛕 Kashi Vishwanath Temple

The Kashi Vishwanath Temple, located in Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh, is one of the holiest Hindu temples dedicated to Lord Shiva.

📜 History – When Was It Built?

  • The temple's history dates back several centuries and has been rebuilt many times due to invasions and destructions.
  • It is one of the twelve Jyotirlingas, the holiest Shiva temples in India.
  • The current structure was built by Ahilya Bai Holkar in the 18th century.
  • It is a major pilgrimage and cultural hub in Varanasi.

🕒 Visiting Information

Timings:
4:00 AM – 11:00 PM daily.
Location:
Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh, India.
Best Time:
October to March, especially during Maha Shivaratri.
