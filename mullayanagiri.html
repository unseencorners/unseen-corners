🏞️ Mullayanagiri – Karnataka’s Highest Peak

Mullayanagiri is the highest peak in Karnataka, located in the Baba Budangiri range, famous for its stunning views and cool climate.

📜 History & Significance

  • Popular trekking destination with scenic landscapes.
  • Known for panoramic sunrise and misty views.
  • Home to unique flora and fauna in the Western Ghats.
  • Important spiritual site with a shrine at the summit.

🕒 Visiting Information

Best Trekking Season:
October–February.
Location:
Chikmagalur, Karnataka, India (Western Ghats).
Activities:
Trekking, photography, bird watching.
