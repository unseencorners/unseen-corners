🏔️ Saltoro Kangri – The Towering Peak of the Saltoro Range

Saltoro Kangri, at 7,742 m (25,400 ft), is the highest peak in the Saltoro Mountains, a subrange of the Karakoram range, located near the Siachen Glacier region.

📜 History & Significance

  • First ascent in 1962 by a joint Japanese-Pakistani expedition.
  • Strategically important due to its proximity to the Siachen Glacier conflict zone.
  • Known for its remote location and challenging climbs.
  • Part of a rugged and lesser-explored mountain region.

🕒 Visiting Information

Best Trekking Season:
July–September.
Location:
Gilgit-Baltistan, Pakistan (Saltoro Range).
Activities:
Mountaineering, glacier trekking, high-altitude exploration.
