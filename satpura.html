🌄 Satpura Hills – Nature’s Hidden Retreat

The Satpura Hills in central India are a lush range of forested mountains rich in biodiversity, serenity, and tribal heritage.

📜 History & Significance

  • Known for Satpura National Park and tiger reserves.
  • Home to ancient tribal communities and sacred groves.
  • Offers pristine forests, sandstone peaks, and river valleys.
  • Perfect for eco-tourism and nature exploration.

🕒 Visiting Information

Best Season:
October to March.
Location:
Madhya Pradesh, Central India.
Activities:
Safari, hiking, wildlife photography, cultural tours.
