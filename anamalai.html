🏞️ Anamalai Hills – Nature's Hidden Haven

The Anamalai Hills, located in Tamil Nadu and Kerala, are part of the Western Ghats and known for rich biodiversity, tribal culture, and scenic landscapes.

📜 History & Significance

  • Home to Anamalai Tiger Reserve and multiple sanctuaries.
  • Inhabited by indigenous tribal communities like the Malasar and Pulayar.
  • Features lush tea and coffee estates from colonial times.
  • Known for waterfalls, dense forests, and rare wildlife species.

🕒 Visiting Information

Best Season:
October to April.
Location:
Tamil Nadu & Kerala, India.
Activities:
Wildlife safaris, tribal village visits, trekking, waterfalls.
