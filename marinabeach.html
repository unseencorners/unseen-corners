🌊 Marina Beach, Chennai

Marina Beach is one of the longest urban beaches in the world, stretching along the Bay of Bengal in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. It’s a perfect blend of natural beauty, cultural heritage, and coastal charm.

🏖️ Highlights & Activities

  • Enjoy peaceful sunrise walks and scenic ocean views.
  • Relish street foods like sundal, murukku, and bhajji.
  • Visit nearby attractions – MGR and Anna memorials.
  • Witness evening fairs, kites, and pony rides along the shore.

🕒 Visitor Information

Timings:
Open all day (Best: 5 AM – 8 PM)
Location:
Marina Beach Road, Chennai, Tamil Nadu.
Best Time:
November – February (pleasant evenings).
