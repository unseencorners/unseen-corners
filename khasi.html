🏞️ Khasi Hills – Mystical Mountains of Meghalaya

The Khasi Hills in Meghalaya are famed for their lush greenery, unique tribal culture, and beautiful landscapes including living root bridges and waterfalls.

📜 History & Significance

  • Home to the Khasi tribe, known for matrilineal culture.
  • Famous for living root bridges unique to the region.
  • Rich biodiversity with numerous endemic species.
  • Contains several sacred groves and natural wonders.

🕒 Visiting Information

Best Season:
March to June, September to November.
Location:
Meghalaya, India.
Activities:
Trekking, cultural tours, visiting living root bridges, waterfalls.
