🏔️ Mount Everest – The Roof of the World

Mount Everest, at 8,848.86 m (29,031.7 ft), is the highest mountain in the world...

📜 History & Significance

  • First summited in 1953 by Sir Edmund Hillary and Tenzing Norgay.
  • A symbol of human endurance and adventure.
  • Sacred to Tibetan and Nepalese cultures.
  • One of the most challenging expeditions on Earth.

🕒 Visiting Information

Best Climbing Season:
April–May, September–October.
Location:
Nepal–China Border (Himalayas).
Activities:
Trekking, mountaineering, photography.
