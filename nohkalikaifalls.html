🌊 Nohkalikai Waterfalls

Nohkalikai Falls, located near Cherrapunji in Meghalaya, is India’s tallest plunge waterfall. Cascading from a height of over 340 meters, it forms a turquoise pool below surrounded by misty cliffs.

📖 Highlights & Facts

  • At 340 meters, it is India’s tallest plunge waterfall.
  • Located near Cherrapunji, one of the wettest places on Earth.
  • Best seen during the monsoon when water flow is at its peak.

🕒 Visitor Information

Timings:
8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
Location:
Cherrapunji, Meghalaya
Best Time:
June – September (Monsoon Season)
