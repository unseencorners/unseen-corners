🛕 Tirupati Balaji Temple (Tirumala Venkateswara Temple)

The Tirumala Venkateswara Temple in Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh, is one of the most famous and richest temples in the world. Dedicated to Lord Venkateswara (an incarnation of Vishnu), it attracts millions of devotees every year from across the globe.

📜 History – When Was It Built?

  • The temple has ancient origins, believed to be over 1,000 years old.
  • The Alwars (Vaishnava saints) sang hymns in praise of Lord Venkateswara as early as the 9th century.
  • The temple’s grand structures and gopurams were expanded during the Pallava, Chola, and Vijayanagara dynasties (10th–15th centuries).
  • Today, it is managed by Tirumala Tirupati Devasthanams (TTD).

🕒 Visiting Information

Timings:
2:30 AM – 1:30 AM (22 hours daily).
Location:
Tirumala Hills, Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh.
Best Time:
All year, esp. Brahmotsavam (Sept–Oct).
