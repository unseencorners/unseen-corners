🏖️ Puri Beach, Odisha

Puri Beach, located along the Bay of Bengal, is one of India’s most spiritual and scenic coastlines. Known for its golden sands, rolling waves, and proximity to the Jagannath Temple, it’s a perfect blend of culture and relaxation.

🌅 Highlights & Facts

  • Renowned for sunrise and sunset views over the Bay of Bengal.
  • Hosts the famous Puri Beach Festival every year.
  • Perfect for swimming, camel rides, and local seafood.
  • Located close to Jagannath Temple – a major pilgrimage site.

🕒 Visitor Information

Timings:
Open 24 hours (best time: sunrise and sunset).
Location:
Puri, Odisha, India.
Best Time:
November – February (pleasant weather).
