🏖️ Baga Beach, Goa

Baga Beach, located in North Goa, is one of India’s most popular beach destinations...

price is 2200 Rs

🌅 Highlights & Facts

Famous for water sports like parasailing, jet-skiing, and banana rides.
Home to iconic clubs like Tito’s and Britto’s beach shack.
Located close to Calangute Beach and Anjuna Beach.
Perfect for sunsets, seafood, and lively music scenes.

🕒 Visitor Information

Timings: Open 24 hours
Location: North Goa, near Calangute
Best Time: November – February

🗓 Suggested Itinerary

Day 1 - Morning: Relax on the beach and try parasailing or jet-skiing.
Day 1 - Afternoon: Enjoy seafood lunch at Britto’s or St. Anthony’s shack.
Day 1 - Night: Experience the lively club scene at Tito’s Lane.
Day 2 - Morning: Visit nearby Calangute Beach or take a dolphin cruise.
Day 2 - Evening: Watch the sunset and enjoy Goan folk performances.
