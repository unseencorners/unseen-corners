🏞️ Shillong Hills

Nestled in the heart of Meghalaya, the Shillong Hills are famed for their lush landscapes, misty mountains, and cool climate. Known as the “Scotland of the East,” Shillong offers panoramic viewpoints, cascading waterfalls, and a rich Khasi culture.

🌿 Highlights & Attractions

  • Visit Shillong Peak for breathtaking views of the city and valleys below.
  • Explore Elephant Falls and Sweet Falls amidst dense greenery.
  • Stroll through Ward’s Lake and enjoy a peaceful boat ride.
  • Experience local Khasi culture at Don Bosco Museum.

🕒 Visitor Information

Timings:
Open all year; best during daylight.
Location:
Shillong, Meghalaya, India.
Best Time:
October – May for clear skies and cool weather.
