🛕 Ramanathaswamy Temple

Ramanathaswamy Temple, located on Rameswaram Island in Tamil Nadu, is a revered Hindu temple dedicated to Lord Shiva. It is famous for its long corridors, magnificent architecture, and spiritual significance as one of the Char Dham pilgrimage sites.

📜 History – When Was It Built?

  • The temple's history traces back to the 12th century, with significant expansions under the Nayak rulers.
  • It boasts the longest corridor among all Hindu temples in India.
  • Known for its sacred water tanks (teerthams) believed to cleanse sins.
  • A vital part of the Ramayana legend, where Lord Rama worshipped Lord Shiva here.

🕒 Visiting Information

Timings:
6:00 AM – 1:00 PM and 4:00 PM – 9:00 PM daily.
Location:
Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, India.
Best Time:
November to February, during festivals like Maha Shivaratri and Navaratri.
