🌊 Chitrakote Waterfalls

Chitrakote Waterfalls, often called the "Niagara of India," is the widest waterfall in India, located on the Indravati River in Bastar district, Chhattisgarh. The horseshoe-shaped fall cascades from a height of about 30 meters, creating a mesmerizing view, especially during the monsoon.

📖 Highlights & Facts

  • Widest waterfall in India, about 300 meters wide in monsoon.
  • Located near Jagdalpur in the Bastar region.
  • Illuminated at night with colorful lights.
  • Boating is available during the post-monsoon season.
