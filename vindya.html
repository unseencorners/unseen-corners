🏞️ Vindhya Hills – The Ancient Heart of Central India

Vindhya Hills stretch across central India, rich in history, mythology, and stunning landscapes featuring ancient temples, dense forests, and scenic viewpoints.

📜 History & Significance

  • Considered a natural boundary between North and South India.
  • Famous for ancient temples and mythological significance.
  • Home to diverse flora and fauna, including several protected species.
  • Offers panoramic views and tranquil nature spots.

🕒 Visiting Information

Best Season:
October to March.
Location:
Madhya Pradesh & Uttar Pradesh, India.
Activities:
Temple visits, trekking, wildlife spotting, nature photography.
