🌿 Cardamom Hills – Spice-Scented Serenity

Located in Kerala, the Cardamom Hills are a lush green part of the Western Ghats, famous for spice plantations, wildlife, and scenic beauty.

📜 History & Significance

  • Renowned for cardamom, pepper, and tea plantations.
  • Home to Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary and elephants.
  • Crucial biodiversity hotspot of the Western Ghats.
  • Culturally rich region influenced by Kerala traditions.

🕒 Visiting Information

Best Season:
September to March.
Location:
Kerala, Southern India.
Activities:
Plantation tours, wildlife safaris, boating, cultural visits.
