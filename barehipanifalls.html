🌿 Barehipani Waterfalls

Barehipani Waterfalls, nestled in Simlipal National Park, Odisha, is India’s second-highest waterfall. Cascading from about 1,309 feet (399 meters), it drops in two tiers amid dense forest and rich biodiversity.

📖 Highlights & Facts

  • Height: 1,309 feet (399 m) – India’s 2nd highest waterfall.
  • Located inside Simlipal Tiger Reserve, Mayurbhanj district.
  • Formed by the Budhabalanga River.
  • Falls in two major drops amidst lush greenery and wildlife.

🕒 Visitor Information

Timings: 6:00 AM – 6:00 PM
Location: Simlipal National Park, Mayurbhanj, Odisha
Best Time: October – February (Winter)
